What we fix most

Five mistakes that cost new businesses later.

None of these are hard to avoid on day one. All of them are slow, stressful and expensive to untangle once the business is running.

Your domain in someone else's nameA former web designer, friend or relative holds the account, and getting it back takes weeks.
Running the business from a personal inboxCustomers trust it less, and moving years of email later is painful.
One password everywhereWhen one site is breached, every account that shares that password is exposed.
No backup until the laptop diesInvoices, quotes and customer records gone, at the worst possible time.
Paying a fake invoiceA convincing email with "new bank details", and the money is gone.
