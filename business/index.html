        BEFORE
        Before it began

        The lesson from Channel 9

        Working at Channel 9 showed me how much businesses depend on their technology, and how badly they were let down when it failed, especially after hours.

        2005

        Midnight IT, a sole trader

        In 2005 I started Midnight IT as a sole trader, with no team and no safety net. I looked after more than 30 clients on my own, took calls at all hours, worked through nights and weekends, and taught myself everything I needed along the way.

        2AM
        After hours

        Nights and weekends

        Many nights I was the only person standing between a client and a business that couldn’t open the next morning.

        Those years taught me resilience, and what real work ethic means.
        SIX
        As trust grew

        The group is formed

        Clients asked who I’d recommend for websites, CCTV and security, AV and more. Referring them on meant we couldn’t control the quality, so we built those businesses ourselves. That is how the group was formed.
