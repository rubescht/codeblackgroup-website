Our story · since 2005

From one person after hours to six specialist teams.

Working at Channel 9 showed our founder how badly businesses were let down when technology failed, especially after hours. In 2005 he started Midnight IT as a sole trader, looking after more than 30 clients on his own through nights and weekends.

Clients kept asking who to trust for websites, CCTV and security. Referring them on meant losing control of the quality, so we built those businesses ourselves. That's how the group was formed.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to use all six services? +

No. Most businesses start with one service, often IT support or cyber security, and add others when they need them. Everything you add sits under the same plan and the same account manager.

What happens when something goes wrong after hours? +

We provide support and monitoring 24/7, so you call one number and a real person picks it up.

Can I spread the cost? +

Yes. Payment plans are available across every service through our finance partner, grenke. Existing Midnight IT clients also receive discounted pricing on other group services.

How is security handled? +

Every solution the group delivers is checked by Strikeforce Cyber, our security division, before it reaches you.

What does the free consult involve? +

A no-obligation conversation with your account manager about what's happening and what success looks like. They come back with the right specialists and a clear plan.
