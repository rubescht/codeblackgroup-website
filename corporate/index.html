A note from our founder
Our clients are not just clients. They are part of our extended family, and we take care of them.
Rubesch ThananjeyanFounder and Group CEO, Code Black Group

Working at Channel 9 showed me how much organisations depend on their technology, and how badly they are let down when it fails, especially after hours.

In 2005 I started Midnight IT as a sole trader and looked after more than 30 clients on my own. As trust grew, clients asked who I would recommend for websites, CCTV and security. Referring them on meant we couldn't control the quality, so we built those businesses ourselves. That is how the group was formed.
