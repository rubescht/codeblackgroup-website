How we work

Problem first. Product last.

Leading consulting firms don't start with a solution. They define the problem, break it down, prioritise what matters and prove the result. We apply the same discipline to your technology, without the RFP.

  1. 01

    Listen

    An executive briefing and interviews with the people closest to the problem. What's happening, what it costs and what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    We break the problem into its parts and test them against the facts: your environment, risk posture, contracts and how work really gets done.

  3. 03

    Prioritise

    Impact against effort and risk. You see which issues matter most, which are quick wins and what can wait.

  4. 04

    Design

    The right specialists from across the group shape one plan, with options, phases and a security review by Strikeforce Cyber built in.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    One team and one point of accountability, starting with quick wins, with change management for the people who use it.

  6. 06

    Prove it

    Agreed measures reported in plain English to your board, then reviewed so the next priority starts from evidence.

No pitch until we understand the problem.
